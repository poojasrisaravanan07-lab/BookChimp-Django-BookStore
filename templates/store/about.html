About BookChimp

A small team of readers building the bookstore we always wanted.

Our story

Books, curated with care.

BookChimp started as a simple idea — buying a book online shouldn't feel like scrolling through a warehouse catalog. We wanted a store that felt like a good local bookshop: organised, opinionated, and genuinely helpful.

Every title on BookChimp is tagged into clear categories, and our AI reading companion (powered by Google Gemini) gives you an honest, spoiler-free summary before you buy — so you always know what you're getting.
